About Citifrag

An Indian house of fragrance & wellness

Established in 2021, Citifrag India Private Limited creates fragrance, wellness, beauty, cosmetics and herbal products designed to bring trust and calm into everyday life.

Our Story

Rooted in tradition, made for today

Citifrag was founded on a simple belief — that fragrance can be a daily ritual of trust and relaxation. What began with incense and home fragrance has grown into a broader family of wellness, beauty, cosmetics and herbal products.

We honour the timeless role of fragrance in Indian life while embracing modern manufacturing, precise formulation and internationally recognised quality standards. Our trademarked Shrine Darbar Batti and a growing set of fragrance ranges reflect this blend of heritage and craft.
