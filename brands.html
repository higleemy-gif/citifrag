New Range

Bamboo-Less Agarbatti

Bamboo-less agarbatti is a modern, refined form of incense crafted without the traditional bamboo core — resulting in a cleaner burn, richer fragrance release and a more premium sensory experience.

Their uniform structure allows smoother burning, better diffusion and a contemporary aesthetic that aligns with wellness-focused consumers seeking purity, simplicity and elevated daily rituals.

  • Bamboo less — better for nature
  • Made with natural ingredients — no chemicals, no toxins
  • Eco-friendly packaging, handcrafted with care
