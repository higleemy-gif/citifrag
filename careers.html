  • Develop and execute sales strategies to achieve revenue and volume targets.
  • Manage distributors, dealers, retailers, modern trade and institutional customers.
  • Identify and develop new markets, distributors and channel partners.
  • Build strong relationships with distributors, retailers and key customers.
  • Drive primary and secondary sales across assigned territories.
  • Monitor market trends, competitor activities, pricing and customer preferences.
  • Lead sales promotions, product launches and merchandising initiatives.
  • Ensure effective product availability, visibility and distribution.

  • Graduate / MBA in Sales, Marketing, Business Administration or a related field.

  • Strong distributor and channel-management experience.
  • Proven track record of achieving sales targets.
  • Good communication, negotiation and relationship-management skills.
  • Strong understanding of the Gujarat market will be an advantage.
  • Willingness to travel extensively within the assigned territory.
  • Entrepreneurial mindset with the ability to build a market from the ground up.

Preferred: Candidates who have handled FMCG / Cosmetics / Fragrance / Incense products and have an established distributor / retailer network in Gujarat.

Head – Operations

Citifrag India Private Limited

Full-time
  • Experience 10–15 years
  • Location Ahmedabad / Gujarat
  • Industry FMCG · Cosmetics · Incense & Fragrance · Wellness

The Head – Operations will be responsible for overseeing end-to-end operations — ensuring efficient production, quality, supply chain, inventory, logistics and process execution while supporting the company’s growth objectives.

Key responsibilities
  • Lead and manage day-to-day business and operational activities.
  • Plan and oversee production, manufacturing and packing operations.
  • Ensure optimum utilization of manpower, machinery, materials and resources.
  • Develop and implement SOPs, systems and operational processes.
  • Manage procurement, inventory, warehousing and logistics.
  • Ensure timely availability of raw materials and finished products.
  • Coordinate with Sales, Marketing, Finance, Quality and Supply Chain teams.
  • Monitor production plans, operational costs, wastage and productivity.
  • Establish and track operational KPIs and performance dashboards.
  • Ensure compliance with quality, statutory, safety and regulatory requirements.
  • Identify operational bottlenecks and implement corrective and preventive actions.
  • Develop vendors, contract manufacturers and logistics partners.
  • Drive cost optimization and continuous improvement initiatives.
  • Support new product launches and scale-up of business operations.
  • Build, mentor and manage the operations team.
  • Provide regular MIS and operational updates to the management.
Candidate profile
  • Graduate / Postgraduate in Operations, Supply Chain or a related discipline.
  • 10–15 years of experience in operations, preferably in FMCG, Cosmetics, Pharma, Incense, Fragrance, Personal Care or Wellness.
  • Strong experience in manufacturing, supply chain, procurement, inventory and logistics.
  • Experience managing third-party / contract manufacturing will be an advantage.

Preferred background: Candidates who have managed end-to-end operations in FMCG, cosmetics, pharma, fragrance, incense or personal-care businesses, particularly in a growing organization.
