Who We Are

An Indian house built on tradition, craft and modern quality

Citifrag India Private Limited was established in 2021 with a clear purpose — to create fragrance and wellness products that bring a sense of trust and calm into everyday life.

From incense and home fragrance to wellness, beauty, cosmetics and herbal ranges, every product reflects our respect for Indian rituals and our commitment to consistent, certified quality.
